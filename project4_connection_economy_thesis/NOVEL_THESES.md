## Thesis 1: The Bar Replacement — Competitive Social Eatertainment

### The idea

Gen Z is not becoming antisocial. They are rejecting **alcohol-centric, loud, nighttime venues** and replacing them with **play-based, daytime-or-evening, alcohol-optional third spaces**.

This is a **venue operator** thesis — not a beverage thesis.

### Data backbone

| Stat | Value | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Want settings where alcohol isn't the focus | **73%** | Harris Poll 2026 |
| Prefer daytime over nighttime socializing | **54%** | Harris Poll 2026 |
| Avoid loud, crowded spaces | **38%** | Harris Poll 2026 |
| Plan to visit a sober bar | **41%** (vs 22% all adults) | NCSolutions / LSN |
| Net decline in clubbing/dancing vs prior year | **−36%** | Morning Consult June 2025 |
| Net decline in bar-hopping | **−35%** | Morning Consult |

Harris also shows **44% still go to bars/clubs** — this is a **share shift**, not extinction. The winners capture migration, not total social budget.

### Novel stock mapping

| Ticker | Company | Why it fits | Evidence tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PLAY** | Dave & Buster's | 243 NA venues; "Store of Future" with immersive group games + **D&B Unlocked** ticketed sober nightlife (karaoke, themed events, raves); Main Event bowling/laser tag for families | B (Harris + SEC filings) |
| **MODG** | Topgolf Callaway | ~$1.8B competitive socializing venues; consumer 1–2 bay segment SSS **+2.4%** Q3 2025; pending Topgolf spin-off creates pure-play; ranked #1 fun/atmosphere | A (earnings) |
| **FUN** | Six Flags | Daytime group destination; affordable vs concert/bar spend; benefits from "54% prefer daytime" | B (sector inference) |

### Critical evaluation

**Bull case:** PLAY and MODG are **purpose-built** for the Harris Poll profile — low-pressure, activity-first, alcohol-optional.

**Bear case:** PLAY same-store sales have been weak; legacy arcade formats are ** losing to specialized competitive-social venues** (White Hutchinson industry analysis). MODG faced negative full-year same-venue sales in 2025; Puttshack/simulator bars compete on price.

**Verdict:** **Medium conviction** on MODG (operational inflection data); **Medium-High** on thesis direction, **Medium** on PLAY execution.

**Not the same as LYV:** Concerts are episodic, premium, artist-driven. Eatertainment is **repeatable weekly social infrastructure** at $25–60/person.

## Thesis 2: Pet Parent Proxy — Connection Before Children

### The idea

Gen Z delays parenthood but still craves **bonded, daily, physical connection**. Pets fill the "connection slot" with **higher spend intensity** than any prior generation — insurance, vet visits, sports, parties.

This is the **"family formation substitute"** trade.

### Data backbone

| Stat | Value | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Gen Z vet visits "few times/year or when something seems off" | **71%** | Rover 2026 |
| Gen Z pets participate in sports | **30%** | Rover 2026 |
| Gen Z pets attend parties | **24%** | Rover 2026 |
| Under-40 share of **new** pet insurance policies | **~70%** | NAPHIA/APPA 2025 |
| Trupanion enrolled pets | **1,096,173** | Q4 2025 earnings |
| U.S. pet insurance GWP growth 2025 | **+23%** | NAPHIA |

Rover: Gen Z is **least comfortable** leaving pets alone 8+ hours — pets are integrated into daily life rhythm, not accessories.

### Novel stock mapping

| Ticker | Company | Why it fits | Evidence tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TRUP** | Trupanion | Pure-play pet medical insurance; 1M+ pets; Chewy distribution; under-40 dominates new policies | A |
| **CHWY** | Chewy | Digital-native pet parent platform; autoship retention; benefits from premiumization | B |
| **IDXX** | Idexx Laboratories | Vet diagnostic volume from proactive younger pet parents | B |
| **ELAN** | Elanco Animal Health | Pet pharma/wellness; more speculative Gen Z link | C |

### Critical evaluation

**Bull case:** Pet insurance penetration still only **~5.5%** of U.S. pet households — massive runway as Gen Z ages into peak pet ownership.

**Bear case:** TRUP trades near 52-week lows despite revenue beats — **pet acquisition cost inflation**, competition from Lemonade/ManyPets.

**Verdict:** **High conviction on TRUP/CHWY thematic fit**; stock timing separate from thesis.

**Not the same as "wellness stocks":** This is **relationship infrastructure**, not self-care products.

## Thesis 3: Swipe Fatigue — Short the App, Long the Venue (Pair Trade)

### The idea

Gen Z is **exiting the swipe economy** and returning to **activity-mediated meeting** (run clubs, pottery, bowling, pickleball). Dating apps are responding with IRL events — but subscriber data says the pivot may be **too late**.

This is the most **contrarian** thesis in the document.

### Data backbone

| Stat | Value | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Gen Z dating app burnout | **79%** | Forbes Health/OnePoll |
| 18–29 met current partner IRL (not online) | **77%** | Hims survey 2025 |
| Tinder MAU decline Q2 2026 | **−7%** | Match Group earnings |
| Match Group paying users Q4 2025 | **−5%** YoY | Match Q4 2025 |
| Bumble paying users Q3 2025 | **−16%** YoY | Bumble Q3 2025 |
| Gen Z uncomfortable with AI dating features | Higher than millennials | Bloomberg/Attest 2025 |

Tinder is expanding IRL events to **75 cities by end of 2026** (bowling, pottery, raves) — validating the thesis while admitting app-only model is broken.

Pickleball courts grew **~50%** from 2023–2025; hobby-based dating ranked as top 2026 social paradigm shift (Eventbrite, Tinder's own research).

### Novel stock mapping

| Ticker | Company | Position | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MTCH** | Match Group | **Contrarian short / avoid** | 8 straight quarters of subscriber decline; Gen Z = 50% of Tinder MAU but dating less; AI features rejected |
| **BMBL** | Bumble | **Avoid** | −16% paying users; friendship pivot unproven |
| **PLAY** | Dave & Buster's | **Long (overlap Thesis 1)** | Tinder hosts events at bowling/arcade venues — literal infrastructure |
| **MODG** | Topgolf | **Long** | Activity-first dating venue |
| **AFRM** | Affirm | **Long (facilitator)** | 31% Gen Z used BNPL for concert/social tickets — enables IRL when cash-constrained |

### Pair trade structure (illustrative)

```
Long:  PLAY + MODG (IRL meeting infrastructure)
Short: MTCH (declining swipe monetization)
Hedge: AFRM (BNPL for experiences — both apps and venues use it)
```

### Critical evaluation

**Bull case on short:** Category-wide contraction — installs and sessions down 2024–2025 across competitors (Relish analysis).

**Bear case on short:** Tinder still has tens of millions of MAU; IRL events could **re-accelerate** engagement; MTCH is cheap if pivot works.

**Verdict:** **Highest-risk, highest-novelty** idea. Data supports **sector headwind**; timing the short is hard.

## Thesis 4: The Analog Connection Economy — Vinyl, Board Games, Crafts

### The idea

Gen Z is buying **tactile, offline, shareable objects** — not despite digital nativity, but **because of digital exhaustion**. Vinyl, board games, and grandmacore crafts are **connection goods**: they require presence, create ritual, and signal identity.

### Data backbone

| Stat | Value | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| U.S. vinyl revenue 2025 | **$1.04B** (+9.3% YoY) | RIAA |
| Gen Z buy vinyl at least monthly | **76%** (18–24) | Vinyl Alliance 2025 |
| Gen Z shop for records in-store | **84%** | Vinyl Alliance |
| Gen Z want more IRL self-expression | **82%** | Board game revival surveys |
| Board game market sales increase | **~20%** (2024) | Market Research Future |
| Gen Z spend on hobby/class/creative | **54%** allocate weekend budget | Harris 2026 |

Futuresource: **60% of Gen Z buy vinyl**; **28% don't own a turntable** — collectibility and decor matter, not just listening.

### Novel stock mapping

| Ticker | Company | Why it fits | Evidence tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WMG** | Warner Music Group | Direct beneficiary of vinyl revenue ($1B+ industry); physical + streaming dual monetization | A (RIAA) |
| **SONY** | Sony Group | Music division + imaging (cameras for "analog bags" trend) + PlayStation social | A/B |
| **HAS** | Hasbro | Board game/tabletop renaissance; "Playing to Win" aging up to 750M fans; D&D/MTG as **social** games | B |
| **PINS** | Pinterest | Digital-to-physical bridge — craft/DIY/grandmacore inspiration → IRL making | B |

### Critical evaluation

**Bull case:** Analog formats have **19 consecutive years** of vinyl growth; Gen Z is now the **primary driver**, not boomers.

**Bear case:** Vinyl average price up **24%** 2020–2025 (Discogs) — 29% of Gen Z reducing purchases due to cost; streaming still dominates revenue.

**Verdict:** **Medium-High** on WMG/HAS thematic fit. **WMG** is the cleanest public equity pure-play on physical music revival.

**Not the same as ETSY:** Etsy is marketplace for **other people's** handmade goods. WMG/HAS are **IP owners** of the analog ritual itself.

## Thesis 5: Sleep Maxxing — The Anti-Nightlife Trade

### The idea

Gen Z is rebranding as **"Gen Zzz"** — choosing early bedtimes, recovery, and next-day gym performance over hangovers. This is the **inverse of bar/concert nightlife** with a different beneficiary set.

### Data backbone

| Stat | Value | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Gen Z cite mental wellness as top night-out decision factor | **24%** (+8pp vs all adults) | Morning Consult 2025 |
| Feel guilty after night out disrupts fitness | **55.9%** | OriGym UK 2025 |
| Prefer fitness/health over drinking/nightlife | **43%** vs 17% | OriGym UK 2025 |
| Only 18% go out several times/week | vs higher for older gens at same age | OriGym UK 2025 |

TikTok **#earlynight** trend; WFLA documents Gen Z choosing 9pm bedtimes over clubbing.

### Novel stock mapping

| Ticker | Company | Why it fits | Evidence tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TPX** | Tempur Sealy | Mattress/sleep products; sleep-as-performance culture | B |
| **RMD** | ResMed | Sleep health tech; long-term CPAP/wellness monitoring | B |

### Critical evaluation

**Bull case:** Structural shift in **when** Gen Z socializes (daytime) implies **more sleep investment**.

**Bear case:** TPX/RMD skew older demographic today; Gen Z sleep spend may flow to **DTC brands** (Casper private) not public mattress cos.

**Verdict:** **Medium conviction, weak ticker mapping** — thesis direction strong, public equity fit mediocre. Include for completeness; prefer Thesis 1–4 for stock specificity.

## Thesis 6: Home Social Hub — The 74% Staying-In Economy

### The idea

The biggest Gen Z social venue is **the living room**. Harris shows staying in (74%) beats going out to bars (44%). Spend flows to **hosting, delivery, streaming, hobbies** — not tickets.

### Data backbone

| Stat | Value | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| "Staying in" at home — top weekend activity | **74%** | Harris 2026 |
| Allocate budget to hosting/spending time with friends at home | **48%** | Harris 2026 |
| Hobby/class/creative activity spend | **54%** | Harris 2026 |
| Rejecting $200 dates for "solo-maxxing" | Trend | Fortune 2026 |

### Novel stock mapping

| Ticker | Company | Why it fits | Evidence tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WING** | Wingstop | Delivery-forward group food for home hangouts | B |
| **DPZ** | Domino's Pizza | Same; affordable hosting fuel | B |
| **NFLX** | Netflix | 71% watch TV/streaming on weekends — shared watch culture | C (weak causality) |
| **CHWY** | Chewy | Pets at home — overlaps Thesis 2 | B |

### Critical evaluation

**Bull case:** Recession-resilient; aligns with Harris cost workarounds (85% use budget tricks for socializing).

**Bear case:** Low moat, commodity delivery; **not durable "connection economy" alpha**.

**Verdict:** **Medium thematic, low stock conviction** — useful as portfolio context, not core alpha.
